FOR IPHONE AND IPAD

Your music, your way.

Salt Player is a local music player for the collection you already own. Bring your files, organize your library, and listen with lyrics.

Requires iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 and later.

Bring your collection

Add a music folder from Files, transfer audio from a computer on the same local network, or add accessible songs from your system music library.

Listen and organize

Browse your library, create playlists, use background playback and view lyrics. Local playback does not require an account or an Apple Music subscription.

Optional Pro

Unlock features including the equalizer, tag editing, spectrum animation, iCloud sync and CarPlay. Choose a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase; prices are shown in the app.

Support

Need help with Salt Player for iPhone or iPad? Email support@xuncorp.com. Please include your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue.

Do not send your Apple Account password, purchase credentials or entire music library.
